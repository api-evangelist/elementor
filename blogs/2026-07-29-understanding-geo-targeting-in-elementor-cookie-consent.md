The Elementor Cookie Consent plugin helps you manage website cookies and comply with global privacy regulations like GDPR and CCPA. With Geo-Targeting, your consent banner automatically detects a visitor’s location and displays the correct legal posture, ensuring compliance without imposing unnecessary friction on international visitors.
